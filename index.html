// 01 — about

I'm a student who got a BBC micro:bit and never really stopped poking at it. Most of what I make starts as a dumb little idea — "what if this blinked when that happened" — and turns into a real, finished project by the time I'm done overthinking it.

I'm not classically trained in any of this. I read docs, break things, read the docs again, and slowly figure out how the board actually works. Everything on this site is the result of that loop — nothing fancy, just built and shipped.
